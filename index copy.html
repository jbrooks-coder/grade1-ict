▶️ Switch On

To switch on the tablet:

Find the power button.
Press and hold it for 3 seconds.
Watch the screen appear.

⏹️ Switch Off

To switch off the tablet:

Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds.
Tap Power off.
Tap OK.
The tablet shuts down.
